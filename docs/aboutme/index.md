#About Me
Hi! I am Noah Emery and I am a second year mechanical engineering student with a concentration in motorsports. 

I was introduced to NASCAR when I was 7 years old. I loved watching and over time kart racing when I could. However, the idea of being able to race or work in and around cars seemed so far fetched. I grew up without a racing background and with no real way to get into the profession. Most of my early years and my early years of high school I was set on becoming an architect. I loved the idea of designing and letting my mind wander. I loved to design things that were not "correct." Things that made a person look twice. That was until I got deep enough into high school to where I was on an accelerated path. I went to an early college and spent my last two years at a community college. There I had a professor that sat down and told me that someone that is able to become an engineer owes it to everyone else as some are not as gifted. I took that to heart and when I started applying to colleges I applied as an engineer. I got into my dream school of Tennessee as a civil engineering major and originally applied to UNC Charlotte just incase. But I was influenced to tour and that is where I discovered the motorsports engineering program. It was meant to be. I fell in love with the campus and the chance to follow that childhood dream.
