## Objective 

This weeks project was all about coming together as a group and designing a tensile tester with given parameters. We were to focus on the mathematical and 3D design of the lead screw, nut and flange, and crosshead while designing and selecting appropriate products and keep track of the costs. After designing the tester we were tasked with performing FEA on the crosshead to verify that it could support a 2kN force, then creating a drawing of the crosshead.

For the main frame and crosshead we decided to use a t-slotted beam as it is common in designs like these. The design is a complete tensile tester that includes calculated measurements and pieces along with McMaster parts and basic framing.

The FEA produced showed that the crosshead for the design underwent the expected stress, strain, and displacement and was strong enough for the tensile tester. However, as with any design improvements can still be made. For this design in particular improvements could include larger washers or bolts in order to better distribute the load or fixing the extrusions with an epoxy or other resin.

## Lessons Learned

This project was by far one of the largest in terms of workload, however, with the load being split between three people it allowed for in depth analysis, calculations, and teamwork to get to the final product. The engineering process did have its issues along the way. When calculating the needed dimensions the algebra at times got messy. And finding McMaster parts was at times tricky as there are certain specifications that would not match up. The cost tree also had its issues as getting familiar with the coding took some time. Finally, when designing the completed assembly, mating the extrusions was difficult due to them not being completely flat on top. However, working through these issues and communicating with others allowed us to get through the mishaps and create the final design. Overall this assignment taught teamwork and how to work through an entire engineering design process with others along with little lessons along the way when running into issues.
