## Objective

The objective of this project is to create a bracket that can support a horizontal force being applied by a rope. We are given different "fittings" to determine how accurate and important different parts of the bracket should be. We are also given a target safety factor of 4 along with the choice of a 500 to 800 force to be applied. This assignment is meant to teach and demonstrate the importance and overall use of fittings while also using mathematical problem solving and sketches to show the engineering thought process and proof behind the design.
