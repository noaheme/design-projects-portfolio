## Objective

The objective of this project is to create a bracket that can support a horizontal force being applied by a rope. We are given different "fittings" to determine how accurate and important different parts of the bracket should be. We are also given a target safety factor of 4 along with the choice of a 500 to 800 force to be applied. This assignment is meant to teach and demonstrate the importance and overall use of fittings while also using mathematical problem solving and sketches to show the engineering thought process and proof behind the design.

## Communicate: Lessons Learned

Governing Failure Mode:

Feature C is a perfect example of a governing failure mode. In the case of feature C the stress analysis strictly governs the output as it requires almost double the minimum thicness as the stiffness analysis. 

Error Propagation:

Error Propagation can be seen on every feature stemming from feature A. The entire bracket is build onto itself based on other features. It is how most things are made. One specific instance is that the feature A is seen all over the part due to the moment equations used to solve for the other features. This could have came back to bite me if I had used 600lbf instead of the 1200lbf due to the rope pulling on both sides of feature A.

Assumption Sensitivity:

One assumption that I made many times in the project was using a centralized force. This was mainly used to keep the free body diagrams simple and easy to navigate. If this was different or wrong it could have resulted in complex algebra which I would have most likely messed up as I would have had to account for a distributed force.
