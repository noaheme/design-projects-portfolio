# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective

This project shifts away from the CAD design part of the course and focuses on the mathematical design of a pulley. We are given that the pulleys must be 4-6 inches apart while having a rotational speed of 1000-2000 RPM and a fixed belt tension of 4-6 lbf. Other parameters include specific allotted materials as well as a safety factor of 3. The objective beyond the design is to use the engineering mind to solve a problem and make sure that the decided values fit withing a certain window while showing the work and documenting issues and other design challenges along the way.

## Analyze

For this assignment I went with 5cm distance between the 3cm diameter pulleys along with a 1500rpm force and 5lbf of tension. Lastly I chose to make the design out of aluminum as it is a balance of strength, weight, machinability, and cost.

For the first part of this project I was tasked with calculating the necessary dimensions for a belt for the pulleys then choosing the correct belt from McMaster. I started with listing my knowns and unknowns and solving for the unknown variables. I then solved with the known variables and determined that a quarter inch belt with a length of 19 and a quarter inches would work perfectly. I then went to McMaster and found that pulley 3044K606 was perfect.

https://www.mcmaster.com/3044K606-1835T382/

- Shaft Calculations

<img width="1016" height="1314" alt="0" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d52f6775-e84f-4984-b8a3-f6e0d7f3cd64" />

Next I had to calculate what size shaft would be needed to support the pulley system. Following the same steps as the belt calculations I came to the conclusion that a quarter inch diameter shaft made out of 1060 hardened steel would be plenty to support the system. 

- Bearing Calculations

<img width="1016" height="1314" alt="0" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e7b4c6bf-b9b6-4257-bc0b-dcd9fe00ca0d" />

For the last major calculations I had to determine what bearing would go around the shaft and pulley. Then choosing the correct material out of the machinery handbook. After running through the calculation I had plenty of options to choose from. I decided to go with dry graphite as it was strong enough to support the forces yet not so strong that it was wasted.

- Frame Design

<img width="1016" height="1314" alt="0" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9c1999bd-7f86-41a7-90f2-d90fde6896ad" />

After determining the belt, shaft, and bearing needs I made a design for the frame that would be holding the pulley system together. I chose values that made sense to me and also fit within the maximum and minimum values calculated above. I landed on an oval frame with holes for the shaft bearings to support the system. I decided to use 6061-T6 Aluminum as it is fairly standard. I then made sure that the steel would be able to withstand the force from the pulley system.

## Lessons Learned

This assignment was a nice break from having to take a mathematical design and create it in CAD. The ability to use the numbers first hand and come up with a frame idea allowed for an enjoyable and informative project. Overall, it was relatively straight forward with the only hiccups being mathematical errors like using the wrong force for the belt and not being able to find the right size. This project took roughly 2 hours.
