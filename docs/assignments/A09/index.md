# A9 – Pulleys

## Objective

This project shifts away from the CAD design part of the course and focuses on the mathematical design of a pulley. We are given that the pulleys must be 4-6 inches apart while having a rotational speed of 1000-2000 RPM and a fixed belt tension of 4-6 lbf. Other parameters include specific allotted materials as well as a safety factor of 3. The objective beyond the design is to use the engineering mind to solve a problem and make sure that the decided values fit withing a certain window while showing the work and documenting issues and other design challenges along the way.

## Analyze

For this assignment I went with 5cm distance between the 3cm diameter pulleys along with a 1500rpm force and 5lbf of tension. Lastly I chose to make the design out of aluminum as it is a balance of strength, weight, machinability, and cost.
